## Context

Periorbital volumetry, meaning the measurement of tissue volume around the eye socket, needs a method clinicians can trust. The Vectra-H2 3D system promises exactly that, contact free and in seconds. Whether it keeps the promise was an open question.

## Brief

Since 2023 I have led the validation study at Göttingen University Medical Center. Two questions sit at the centre: how accurately does the system measure, and how reproducible are results across captures, examiners and sessions?

## Approach

- Study design, definition of collection criteria and test planning
- Running the measurement series inside live clinical operation
- Statistical assessment of accuracy and reproducibility in Python and Excel, plus data validation
- Systematic root cause analysis of the measurement and inspection processes using Ishikawa and 5-Why, followed by corrective actions
- Working with the clinical users and translating their needs into technical requirements

## Outcome

The work formed the basis of my bachelor's thesis, graded 2.2, and was presented in 2024 at the 73rd meeting of the North German Ophthalmological Society. The study is ongoing.
