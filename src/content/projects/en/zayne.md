## Context

From 2022 to 2026 I ran zayne, my own studio for digital client work. The scope ranged from web and content solutions through brand identity and design to search optimisation.

## Brief

Full responsibility for acquisition, concept, delivery, sign off and invoicing. Over 120 projects, each one from first enquiry to go live.

## Approach

- Eliciting requirements in direct conversation, assessing them qualitatively and quantitatively, translating them into functional requirements
- Structuring requirements into work packages, priorities and dependencies
- Facilitating between client, development and suppliers, resolving conflicting requirements
- Running development roadmaps, schedules and budgets across parallel projects
- Quality assurance and sign off with the client: functional tests, correction rounds, final release

## Outcome

Over 120 completed client projects with full revenue responsibility. Reusable templates and standardised processes measurably shortened turnaround per project.
